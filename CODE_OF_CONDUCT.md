# Code of Conduct

api-anything follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/mtbun/api-anything/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@mtbun](https://github.com/mtbun). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who do not follow these rules.
